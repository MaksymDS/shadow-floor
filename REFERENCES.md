# Figure references

This register links the published figures and their [CSV inputs](shadow06-figdata/) to source publications. A link identifies the publication behind a figure entry; it does not independently validate a detector result, company estimate or legal interpretation. The figures preserve the source's sample, date and unit where available.

## F1 · Web pages

- [Pew Research Center, “How much of the internet is written with AI?”](https://www.pewresearch.org/data-labs/2026/08/20/how-much-of-the-internet-is-written-with-ai/) and its [methodology](https://www.pewresearch.org/data-labs/2026/08/20/methodology-ai-content/). Values are detector estimates from sampled pages; the plotted points are six-month averages.

## F2 · Music

- [Deezer, April 2026 update](https://newsroom-deezer.com/2026/04/ai-generated-tracks-represent-44-of-new-uploaded-music/) and [July 2026 update](https://newsroom-deezer.com/2026/07/ai-music-exceeds-50-percent-daily-uploads-deezer/). These are Deezer's own upload and stream figures. The June peak is recorded as `over 50%`; no upload share is inferred for January 2025.

## F3 · arXiv

## F4 · Institutional decisions

- [Pew study](https://www.pewresearch.org/data-labs/2026/08/20/how-much-of-the-internet-is-written-with-ai/).
