# Figure references

This register links the published figures and their [CSV inputs](shadow06-figdata/) to source publications. IDs such as `A01` and `F30` in the data identify source records. A link identifies the publication behind a record; it does not independently validate a detector result, company estimate or legal interpretation. The figures preserve the source's sample, date and unit where available.

## F1 · Web pages

- **A01–A05:** [Pew Research Center, “How much of the internet is written with AI?”](https://www.pewresearch.org/data-labs/2026/08/20/how-much-of-the-internet-is-written-with-ai/) and its [methodology](https://www.pewresearch.org/data-labs/2026/08/20/methodology-ai-content/). Values are detector estimates from sampled pages; the plotted points are six-month averages.

## F2 · Music

- **A26–A28:** [Deezer, April 2026 update](https://newsroom-deezer.com/2026/04/ai-generated-tracks-represent-44-of-new-uploaded-music/) and [July 2026 update](https://newsroom-deezer.com/2026/07/ai-music-exceeds-50-percent-daily-uploads-deezer/). These are Deezer's own upload and stream figures. The CSV's June share is `50`; the source describes the peak as greater than 50%.

## F3 · arXiv

- **F01–F06:** [arXiv, updated rate-limit policy and submission counts](https://blog.arxiv.org/2026/10/01/updated-rate-limit-policy/). Counts and policy description come from arXiv's post.

## F4 · Institutional decisions

- **A01–A05:** [Pew study](https://www.pewresearch.org/data-labs/2026/08/20/how-much-of-the-internet-is-written-with-ai/).

- **E14–E15:** [University of Waterloo announcement](https://uwaterloo.ca/associate-vice-president-academic/news/waterloo-discontinuing-use-ai-detection-tool-turnitincom) and [Curtin University announcement](https://www.curtin.edu.au/news/oasis-news/update-on-turnitin-ai-detection-tool/).

- **E20:** [*Matter of Newby v Adelphi University*, New York court opinion](https://www.nycourts.gov/reporter/3dseries/2026/2026_26021.htm). The figure describes this case's outcome, not a general ruling on detectors.
- **Figure note:** [Liang et al., ICML 2024](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/liang24b.html) presents a corpus-level estimation method, distinct from deciding whether an individual document was AI-authored.
